## Provisional Agent Evidence

An independent agent grader reviewed 10 anonymized predictions against the frozen reference. Revision 2 reports 7 supported, 0 unsupported, and 3 unresolved predictions. The provisional split is A: 4 supported and 1 unresolved; B: 3 supported and 2 unresolved. All 10 predictions have valid line references, which establishes citation locations only, not semantic correctness.

The grader changed p0006 from supported to unresolved after checking the explicit recheck contract and conservative ambiguity tests. A unique perfect matching candidate did not establish that the current finding represented the same root-cause instance. The v1 result remains preserved outside this repository; only revision 2 is summarized in the evidence export.

The two frozen known candidates each have at least one matching agent prediction: case 1's evidence-free recheck resolution (p0001/p0003) and case 3's global EOF behavior change (p0004/p0005). This is provisional agent-only candidate coverage, not human known-defect recall.

The three unresolved predictions mark distinct evidence limits. p0006 follows the conservative matching contract: the documented rules do not require a global maximum-matching assignment, and the captured paths do not prove root-cause identity. p0007 identifies an unbounded prompt-summary path that reached about 1.2 million characters in a synthetic probe, but the deployed model's input limit is unknown. p0010 identifies a possible HTTPX pool-saturation failure with a 10-connection cap and 2-second pool timeout, but expected concurrency and request latency are unknown. None is a confirmed defect.

This pilot cannot attribute any review gap to context omission: reported read paths were not independently audited, and the context-selector condition was not run. The next step is to obtain human labels, complete host/model/version/configuration and Skill-load metadata, audit or capture coverage traces, then repeat the paired conditions. A selector condition should follow only if that evidence shows context gaps worth targeting.

These decisions are agent evidence, not human labels. Unresolved predictions are not false positives. No human label file has been accepted for any case; human precision, recall, clean-case false alarms, and wrong-resolution counts all remain null.

## Limits

- The host outputs do not include full transcripts or dependable model/version/usage/cost data.
- Reported read paths are claims; review coverage was not independently audited.
- The benchmark's reviewer access boundary was prompt-restricted, not filesystem-isolated.
- Ten cases come from one repository and one from Click, so external generalization is limited.
- The sample has 11 case groups and cannot support broad claims about Skill quality.
